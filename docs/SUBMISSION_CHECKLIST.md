## Devpost description draft
**PlanetCraft: Galactic Survival** is an offline voxel survival sandbox for Android, stitched
together from a voxel building game, a space exploration game, a factory sim, an action RPG loot
system and a terraforming simulation, all in one 29 MB APK. Every texture, icon, creature, sound
and music track is generated by the game's own code. Built with Godot 4.7 and Kiro CLI: steering
docs pinned the constraints (offline, original, mid-range Android at 30+ FPS), specs drove the
design, and Kiro verified its own work with a headless test suite, a gameplay bot that screenshots
the game, CI and real-device runs over adb.
