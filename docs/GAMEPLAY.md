# Gameplay guide

## The first hour on Verdania
1. You start at a crash pod: an oxygen generator (breathable bubble), med bay (respawn point),
   solar panel, battery and crate. ECHO, your suit AI, narrates the main story.
2. Mine basalt and rust-speckled **ferrite ore** with the mining laser (hold MINE).
   Your best tool works from any inventory slot.
3. Craft a **Fabricator** by hand (BAG → Fabricate) and place it. Stations unlock recipes when
   you stand within 5 blocks of them.
4. Follow the waypoint to **Haven Outpost** and talk to Ossa (trader), Rook (engineer) and
   Dr. Vela (xenobiologist). They give side jobs, and Ossa trades for credit chips.
5. Build a Smelter, smelt ingots, make circuits and a Plasma Pistol before your first night.
   Umbral Stalkers hunt in the dark.

## Survival
| Stat | Drains when | Restore with |
|---|---|---|
| Oxygen | Outside a powered O2 generator bubble (faster underwater) | O2 Canister, O2 zone, rover cabin, a terraformed atmosphere |
| Energy | Jetpack, mining, weapons, helmet lamp at night | Daylight on the suit, power cells, standing near a battery |
| Food | Slowly over time | Rations, veggies, cooked steaks, fruit |
| Temperature | Hot/cold planets, night, blizzards | Heater, O2 zone, shelter, Thermal Lining |
| Radiation | Pyrrhos, Nullara, the Kessler Belt, pollution | Shelter, Rad Shielding, med kits |

At zero oxygen or food, or with extreme temperature/radiation, you lose health. On death you are
reconstructed at the nearest med bay and keep your inventory.

## Colony and automation
- **Power:** solar panels (6 kW in daylight), fusion reactors (40 kW), battery banks store energy.
  Machines stop when the grid has no power.
- **Hydroponic tray:** grows veggies every 75 s. **Miner bay:** a drone flies out and mines an ore
  block within 12 blocks every 14 s. **Smelter:** load ore and it smelts by itself.
- Machines keep producing while you are away on another world (capped at 8 hours).
- Aim at a machine and tap USE to open its panel.

## Creatures, elites and loot
- Passive grazers (Mossback), skittish fliers (Veil Glider), territorial biters (Skitter, Frostmaw),
  hunters (Umbral Stalker at night, Magmite, Dune Wyrm, Void Ray), precursor Sentinels near ruins,
  Hive Drones from alien hives, and the Void Guardian boss on Nullara.
- **Elite ★** variants (7% of spawns) are larger and tougher, roll rare drops at 4× the normal
  chance and have a 30% chance to drop a Stellar Shard.
- Rarity colours: common (white), uncommon (green), rare (blue), epic (purple, with a light beam),
  legendary (orange, with a light beam).
- Rare drops craft unique gear: Umbral Fang Blade, Prism Beam Rifle, Sentinel Arc Cannon,
  Magma Launcher, Frostguard Plating, Wyrmscale Thrusters.
- The **Bestiary** (BAG → Journal) shows species you met, kill counts and discovered drop rates.
- Tame a Mossback with a Bio Lure; it follows you and fights hostiles.

## Structures and events
| Structure | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Precursor ruins | All terrain worlds | Break the Ancient Cache for loot. Sentinels may wake up. |
| Precursor Obelisk | Most worlds | Tap USE on the Obelisk Core: survive three waves of Sentinels to raise a Trial Reliquary. |
| Crashed freighter | Most worlds, Kessler Belt | Break Salvage Crates for parts and fuel. |
| Alien hive | Verdania, Aridus, Pyrrhos | Breaking hive blocks releases drones. The Hive Heart drops rare loot. |
| Starfall shrine | Verdania, Nullara | At night, tap USE on the altar. Falling stars rain shards and rare items for 40 s. |

Weather rolls every few minutes: dust/ash storms and blizzards hurt you outside shelter,
meteor showers leave craters with Meteorite ore, gravity anomalies make jumps floaty.

## Space travel
Place a **Launch Pad**, aim at it and install a hull frame, an engine and a nav computer.
Engine tier decides reachable worlds: tier 1 (Kryos, Aridus, Kessler Belt), tier 2 (Pyrrhos),
tier 3 (Nullara). Each jump burns one Hydrazine Fuel Cell. Every world keeps its own edits,
machines and evolution state.

## Planet Evolution and terraforming
Mining raises **pollution** (grey sky). A powered **Terraformer** raises **atmosphere**, then
**water** (rain appears), then **life** after you insert a Gaia Seed Vault. Atmo Catalysts speed it
up. Around each terraformer, ground turns to soil and grass and grass tufts grow. At 100%
atmosphere the planet becomes breathable.

## Stellar Store
Title screen → STELLAR STORE. Spend Stellar Shards on suit colours, laser colours, rover and ship
paint and starter kits that apply to new expeditions. Shards are rare: elites, bosses, precursor
relics, obelisk trials and starfalls. You can also refine a Crown of the Void or a Precursor Relic
into shards. Everything is offline and permanent.
